How it runs

  • Stet builds the standard

    A research agent assembles your brand's voice from what's already public — principles, vocabulary, the lines that work and the ones that don't. An engagement starts with a documented standard instead of a blank page.

  • Stet drafts against it

    Stet writes copy to formats brands actually use — social, email, audio, web, wherever the brief goes — then grades it against the standard before a creative director sees it. Review time goes to judgment calls, not obvious misses.

  • The Creative Director decides

    Every draft lands in a queue. Approve it, revise it, or kill it and say why. Nothing ships without a person putting their name on it.

  • The call sticks

    Each CD decision traces back to the principle behind it, so the standard sharpens with every review cycle instead of the reasoning disappearing into a thread nobody reads twice. The tool improves, just like a creative department.
